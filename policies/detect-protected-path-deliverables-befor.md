---
type: policy
title: Detect protected-path deliverables before red
description: Escalate to a person early when a task's whole deliverable lies in a protected path, instead of running the red/green cycle.
---
At triage or plan, check where the task's deliverable will live. If all of it lies in a protected path, which the writer cannot legitimately modify, escalate to a person at that point. Do not start red and green. Say in the escalation which protected path is involved and why the task can't be completed without a person. If only part of the deliverable is protected, continue with the unprotected part and flag the protected part. Why: a task whose deliverable the writer can never produce will fail however many iterations it gets. In F-2, triage, red, and two green iterations were spent before the task was cancelled.
